| Story | Points | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Borrower status clarity | 3 | Read-only summary and next step |
| Borrower notice interpretation | 5 | Needs plain-language explanation and callouts |

| Case detail and notes | 8 | Largest servicing surface with timeline, notes, and actions |

## Pointing Rule

Points stay small because this is a POC, not a production release. If a story starts to require hidden branching, split it before estimating.
