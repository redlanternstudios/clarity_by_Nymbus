# Post Release Support

## Hypercare

- Launch enters a defined hypercare window
- Monitoring is tighter during rollout
- Support volume and routing are watched closely

## Internal Chatbot

- Classifies incoming tickets
- Suggests routing destination
- Flags ambiguous or high severity cases
- Does not fully resolve complex issues

## Routing Targets

- Floor support queue
- Customer support queue
- Product review queue
- Technical escalation queue

## Sustainment

- Product owns issue prioritization
- Support owns daily handling
- Engineering owns defect follow-up
- Leadership owns launch visibility

## Exit Criteria

- Ticket routing is stable
- No critical blockers remain open
- Teams understand the support model
- Hypercare can safely step down
