# User Stories

## Borrower

### 1. Status clarity
As a borrower, I want to see my current payment change, loan status, recent notice, and next step at a glance so I do not have to call support for basic updates.

Scenario: Maya opens the borrower home view, sees the payment change and recent notice, and understands what to do next without reading internal servicing terms.

### 2. Notice interpretation
As a borrower, I want Clarity to explain a notice in plain language so I can understand what changed and why.

Scenario: Maya opens Notice Detail and sees highlighted text, callouts, and a plain-language explanation grounded in the notice.

### 3. Ask Clarity
As a borrower, I want to ask a question about my loan or notice and get a grounded answer so I can self-serve with confidence.

Scenario: Maya asks why her payment changed, sees a source-backed answer, and can continue to the notice or escalate if needed.

### 4. Escalation and history
As a borrower, I want to request human help and track the request so I know it was received.

Scenario: Maya submits an escalation reason, gets a reference number, and can view it in history.

## Servicing and Support

### 5. Case queue triage
As a servicing agent, I want to scan the case queue with filters, owner, confidence, and SLA risk so I can prioritize the right work quickly.

Scenario: Jordan filters the queue, opens the highest-risk case, and sees why it surfaced.

### 6. Case detail and notes
As a servicing agent, I want to see full case context and add notes without leaving the case so I can resolve work faster.

Scenario: Jordan reviews the borrower question, adds a note, reassigns ownership, and sees the updated case state.

### 7. Floor support routing
As floor support, I want the chatbot to suggest a route and require confirmation so I can send cases to the right human owner.

Scenario: A launch ticket comes in, Clarity suggests product review, and the floor analyst confirms or overrides it.

### 8. Leadership visibility
As leadership, I want to see trends, launch health, and bottlenecks so I can tell whether the rollout is stable.

Scenario: Leadership reviews volume, queue health, and notice insights to see where friction is concentrated.

### 9. Release package traceability
As product or delivery, I want the requirements, design, stories, and UI prompt to stay aligned so the build is reviewable and defensible.

Scenario: The team uses the repo, Kiro spec, and Drive package together without contradictory labels or routes.
