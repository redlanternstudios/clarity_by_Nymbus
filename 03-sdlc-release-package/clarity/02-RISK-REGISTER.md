# Risk Register

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner | Trigger | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Clarity is mistaken for a replacement system | High | Medium | Keep persistent read-only labeling and explicit boundary copy | Product | Review feedback suggests replacement behavior | Open |
| The portal shell drifts into a role-card marketing page | High | Medium | Keep the shell portal-first with borrower as the default path | Design | Role chooser becomes the dominant landing view | Open |
| Routing destinations start multiplying | High | Medium | Lock final routes to floor support, customer support, product review, and technical escalation | Product | A new route label appears in a screen or doc | Open |
| Case queue actions look interactive but do nothing | High | Medium | Verify every menu item, button, and row action has a visible outcome | Engineering | A user clicks a row action and nothing changes | Open |
| Chatbot language overpromises autonomy | High | Medium | Limit AI copy to suggest, classify, explain, and require human confirmation | Product | Copy implies auto-resolution or auto-close | Open |
| Mock data drifts across screens | High | Medium | Use one canonical dataset for all pages, drawers, and states | Delivery | Counts, names, or statuses differ by screen | Open |
| Repo, Kiro, and Drive drift apart | Medium | High | Update all three locations together and note the source of truth | Delivery | One location changes without the others | Open |
| Hypercare has no exit criteria | High | Medium | Define launch thresholds, ownership, and handoff conditions before rollout | Support | Support volume or routing errors stay unresolved | Open |
